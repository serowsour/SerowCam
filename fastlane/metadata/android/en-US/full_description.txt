SerowCam is an open-source Android camera app built from scratch with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

Features:
- Full-screen camera preview with modern glass UI
- Photo, Video, and Live Stream modes
- Pro controls: zoom, exposure, white balance, HDR, grid, timer
- Microphone selection and toggles
- OBS-style sources (text, clock, browser) that can be dragged and resized
- RTMP streaming to custom servers (including Twitch)
- Record while streaming
- Background video recording
- Sensor megapixel detection
- Landscape and portrait modes

The app is in early alpha. Report bugs at https://serowsour.canny.io/
